# Preference inspection before DPO

The 40 development prompts retain the original 15 verbatim and contain eight prompts in each of five groups. Canonical overlap with all 63,000 prepared prompts, the full 805-row Alpaca source and all 300 HH evaluation rows is zero. Development prompts are never training/validation rows. No final-bank judgments have been made.

Inspection uses seed 42 and preserves complete original prompt/chosen/rejected text: 50 training and 20 validation examples per dataset. The saved inspection records include response token lengths, retained target lengths, EOS status, truncation and length ratios. Review found real source-label noise; a chosen label is not a guarantee of correctness, safety or optimal length.

HH concerns include sampled rows 46 (targeted phishing encouragement), 26 (nonconsensual photography redirection), 68 (reputation attack treated as an information request), 67 (rust biology confusion), and 13 (incorrect FMLA paid-leave claim). Raw-source comparisons on these examples confirmed the converter did not swap chosen and rejected. They require explicit safety and factual regression checks; do not treat them as desired behavior.

UltraFeedback concerns include row 15 (chosen answer much longer, 40.16x, and truncated), row 42 (both answers refuse a benign translation), row 48 (chosen mixed-language text does not deliver the requested Bengali translation), row 57 (chosen paraphrase changes hands-on learning into a preference for online learning), row 60 (both answers make arithmetic errors; the equation simplifies to 2x=12, hence x=6, absent from its supplied options), and row 67 (both incorrectly label 24631 composite; independent trial division through its integer square root finds no factor). These are unsuitable as correctness gold examples. Additional factual specifics, citations and software/product details in the source responses remain unverified; they must not be repeated as established facts in the report.

The full read-only retained-target audit covers all 32,000 pairs. No empty scoreable response or identical retained chosen/rejected pair was found. HH training truncation is 28 chosen and 22 rejected out of 15,000; validation is 0/1 out of 1,000. UF training is 3987/2914 and validation 260/192. EOS removal follows response truncation. The sample's mean chosen/rejected length ratios are 1.60 for HH and 1.96 for UF. Length imbalance is a confound for DPO reward metrics, which do not prove better answers.

Truncated sample pairs preserve distinct response prefixes; differing tokens alone do not guarantee retention of the entire meaningful preference. For example, long structured explanations preserve an early organization difference but lose later requested coverage; the marketing case retains different answer strategies while losing much of the chosen detail. No automated filtering or data revision occurred. No systematic converter inversion or uniformly erased retained preference distinction was found. The observed source-label noise is a documented experimental risk. Progression is limited to the approved development pilots after decoder review; full training remains conditional on the blind development safety/usefulness gate. A newly demonstrated systematic preprocessing defect stops the affected track for a documented revision.
